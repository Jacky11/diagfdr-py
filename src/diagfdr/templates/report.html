Decoy ratio: a consistency check of the exported q-values on the reported list (expected ≤ α by construction), not the realised error. FDP-CV benchmark √2·Dα-1/2: an independence reference for the dispersion of the realised FDP across experiments, neither a confidence interval nor a bound for this list. IPE: decoy-reference PEP concordance error (target mean PEP vs Db/Tb per PEP bin), not a calibration against the truth. πD: decoy fraction among entries with q in [{{ params.low_conf[0] }}, {{ params.low_conf[1] }}]; evaluable only for single-pass paired competition.
